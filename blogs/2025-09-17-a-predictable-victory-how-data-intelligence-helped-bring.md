A Win Years in the Making After years of bidding, the Green Bay Packers and Discover Green Bay, alongside PMI Entertainment Group, secured one of the most coveted events in sports—the 2025 NFL Draft. While passion and persistence fueled the bid, the true game-changer was the use of predictive visitor intelligence to demonstrate impact and prepare the community for success.
