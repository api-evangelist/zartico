International Focus International visitation to the United States looks fundamentally different than it did a year ago. For the first time in over two decades (outside of the pandemic), Mexico has surpassed Canada as the top source of inbound travelers—a shift that signals more than just changing numbers. It reflects evolving travel patterns, economic pressures, and geopolitical realities that destinations across the country are navigating in real time.
